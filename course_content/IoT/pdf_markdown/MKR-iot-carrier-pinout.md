<::Diagram showing the wiring connections for an Arduino IoT Carrier board with various peripherals. The main board is a circular dark grey PCB with "ARDUINO®" labeled at the top. In the center is a light blue Arduino MKR board. To its right is an 18650 Li-Ion 3.7 V battery. Below the battery is an SD Card reader slot. The diagram details connections for power, Grove modules, and relay connectors. To supply with battery, plug the battery connector over the MKR board to the battery connector over the IoT Carrier. MKR Board connections: +BAT connects to VBAT (red wire) and -BAT connects to GND (black wire). On the left side, there are three Grove connectors: GROVE I2C, GROVE A5, and GROVE A6. GROVE I2C connections: GND (pin 4), +5V (pin 3), D11 to SDA (pin 2), D12 to SCL (pin 1). GROVE A5 connections: GND (pin 4), +5V (pin 3), A5 D20 to GROVE_AN5 (pin 1). GROVE A6 connections: GND (pin 4), +5V (pin 3), A6 D21 to GROVE_AN6 (pin 1). At the bottom left, there are two Relay Connectors. A warning states: MAXIMUM voltage of 30V for each Relay Connector. RLY_2 connections: DL3-L2, D13 to RELAY2. RLY_1 connections: DL2-L1, D14 to RELAY1. On the right side, the SD Card reader connections are detailed: Pin 2 to CD/DAT3/SS (D0), Pin 3 to CMD/COPI (D8), Pin 6 to CLK/SCK (D9), Pin 7 to DAT0/CIPO (D10).: figure::>
