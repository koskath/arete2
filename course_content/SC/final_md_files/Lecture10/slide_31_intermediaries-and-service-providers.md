# Intermediaries and Service Providers

Intermediaries or Middlemen | Hybrid Forms | Service Providers

- CI | Commercial Intermediaries: i.e. sales agents, brokers on commodity markets

  ← export merchants, commission houses, trading companies →

  CP | Commercial Service Providers: i.e. for warehousing or customizing, repackaging

- FI | Financial Intermediaries: confirming houses, brokers, traders, sales agents

  ← commercial banks, insurance companies →

  FP | Financial Service Providers: i.e. SWIFT, actors on financial and/or insurance markets

- TI | Transport Intermediaries: i.e. customs house brokers, cargo sales agents, NVOCCs

  ↓

  international freight forwarders, cargo consolidators

  ↑

  TP | Transport Service Providers: i.e. trucking companies, ocean carriers, airlines

- LI | Logistics Intermediaries: i.e. 4PL, system integrators

  ↓

  international freight forwarders, cargo consolidators

  ↑

  LP | Logistics Service Providers: i.e. 3PL, public warehouses, export packaging firms

Source: Kummer et al. (2009), Schramm (2012)
