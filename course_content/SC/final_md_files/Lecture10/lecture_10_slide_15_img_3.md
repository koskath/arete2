The referenced image highlights the term "W/M," which stands for "Weight or Measurement." In supply chain analytics, this concept is crucial for determining shipping costs. Carriers often use volumetric charging, where costs are based on the greater of the actual weight or the volume of the shipment. This approach encourages efficient packaging, optimizing space usage in transport. By integrating this into supply chain analytics, companies can better forecast shipping costs, enhance space utilization, and improve logistical efficiency. Balancing weight and volume impacts decisions on shipment sizes and packaging designs, directly influencing overall supply chain management and cost optimization strategies.
